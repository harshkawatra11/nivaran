# Nivaran

## Register
product

## Users and purpose
Delhi residents reporting civic problems on phones, and legal-aid volunteers following repeated failures. Help them prepare a clear complaint, identify its responsible office, record filing, and track the next escalation. Citizens submit filings themselves.

## Brand personality
Humane, measured, accountable. Preserve the approved paper-and-ink civic identity from the original execution plan.

## Design principles
Show the next action. Make every decision inspectable. Distinguish live results, samples, and unverified sources. Keep technical details in the transparency view.

## Accessibility and inclusion
Readable mobile layouts, visible keyboard focus, labeled controls, reduced motion, and Hindi/English complaint input. Never depend on color alone for status.
